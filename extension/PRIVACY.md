# SoundBar Tabs — Privacy Policy

SoundBar Tabs does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data. Period.

- **No analytics, no tracking, no network requests.** The extension never
  contacts any server.
- **What it accesses:** the list of tabs currently playing audio (title,
  icon, and mute state), so it can show them in the popup, and the pages'
  media elements, so it can change their volume or pause them when you ask.
- **What it stores:** your per-tab volume choices, kept in the browser's
  session storage on your device only. This data is deleted automatically
  when you close the browser.

The full source code is public and auditable at
https://github.com/malenitaa/soundbar.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/malenitaa/soundbar/issues
